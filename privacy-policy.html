Privacy Policy for Society Ledger

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Society Ledger (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) helps residents and authorised society representatives manage society, member and flat information and maintenance-related records. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and retained when you use Society Ledger.

This policy should be read together with the permissions and notices shown in the app. The information processed in a particular use of the app depends on the features you use and the information you or your society provide.

1. Information we may process

  • Account and sign-in information: information used to create, authenticate, and manage your account, such as an account identifier and available profile details.
  • Society and residence information: society, building, flat/unit, membership, and resident details supplied by you or an authorised society administrator.
  • Maintenance and payment records: dues, charges, amounts, payment status, dates, receipts, and related accounting records entered or made available through the app. Do not assume the app processes card or bank credentials unless a payment feature explicitly asks for them.
  • Contact information: phone number and email address, where supplied or made available for account, society, or service communications.
  • Photos and documents: images, receipts, or other files that you choose to capture, select, or upload using an app feature.
  • Location: device location may be accessed if you grant permission and use a feature that requires it. You can manage location permission in your device settings; some location-dependent features may then be unavailable.
  • Push notification information: a device or push-notification token and notification preferences may be processed to deliver app alerts.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: limited device, app, log, or diagnostic information may be processed by the app or its service providers to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • create and secure accounts and authenticate users;
  • maintain society, member, flat, maintenance, dues, and payment-status records;
  • display or share relevant records with authorised residents, society administrators, or other permitted users;
  • process files and documents you choose to submit;
  • send important service, account, maintenance, or payment-status notifications;
  • provide requested location-dependent features;
  • prevent misuse, investigate issues, maintain service reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

3. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

  • to authorised members or administrators of your society when needed to provide society-management functions;
  • to infrastructure, authentication, file-storage, notification, or other service providers that help operate the app, subject to appropriate safeguards;
  • when required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or to protect users, the service, or others;
  • with your direction or consent.

Society Ledger is not a substitute for checking who can access a society record. Please avoid uploading information that is unnecessary for the relevant task and do not share credentials.

4. Payments

The app may display or maintain maintenance dues and payment-related records. If a payment is handled by a separate payment provider, that provider may collect and process information under its own privacy policy. The information collected depends on the payment flow presented to you. We do not intend this policy to imply that Society Ledger stores full card numbers, banking passwords, or payment authentication credentials.

5. Permissions, photos, documents, and location

Camera, media, file, or location access is used only when you invoke a feature that needs the relevant permission. You can revoke permissions in your device settings. Revoking a permission may prevent the related feature from working, but it does not necessarily delete information previously submitted to the service.

6. Data retention and deletion requests

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain society and financial records, resolve disputes, meet legal or accounting obligations, and protect the service. Retention periods may differ by record type and the role of your society.

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, email rentitease@gmail.com from the address associated with your account where possible. Include “Society Ledger privacy request” in the subject and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting. Some records may need to be retained where lawfully required or where they form part of society accounting records.

Uninstalling the app or removing a permission does not itself delete server-side records. If the app offers account deletion within its interface, you may also use that process.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your device and account credentials and report suspected unauthorised access to the contact below.

8. Children’s privacy

Society Ledger is intended for society-management use and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Third-party services and links

The app may rely on third-party services for functions such as authentication, hosting, notifications, storage, diagnostics, or payment processing. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. This policy does not replace those providers’ policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
